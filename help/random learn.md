# random learn
Used for determining the result of a learning attempt
Based on a D20 roll with a default DC of 11

+/- raise/lower your DC respectively (so + is actually detrimental).
Advantage and disadvantage make you roll more times. Under advantage, you take the highest roll; under disadvantage, you take the lowest.

Flags:
- **A**lready learned previously (advantage)
- **n**atural predisposition (-4)
- **g**uidance after a failure (-3)
- **e**xtension of an existing skill (-2)
- on a **s**timulant (-2)
- on an **p**sychedelic/opioid (+2)
- **d**runk/on a depressant (+3)
- **t**emporary mental impairment (+3)
- **i**njury to the brain (+5)
