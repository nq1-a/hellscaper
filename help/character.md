# character
Different characters have different traits and abilities that affect how well they will do at different tasks. To account for that, characters have five stats tied to them:
- **Agility**, or a character's control over their movement and how quickly they can respond to stimuli;
- **charisma**, or how favorably they present themselves to other people;
- **intelligence**, or breadth of knowledge combined with adeptness at analytical thinking;
- **resillience**, or natural resistance to various types of harm;
- and **strength**, or the ability of a character's muscles to exert force.
For any given character, each stat can range from -4 to 4:
- -4: Unable
- -3: Bottom 0.2%
- -2: Bottom 5%
- -1: Bottom 25%
- +0: Median
- +1: Top 25%
- +2: Top 5%
- +3: Top 0.2%
- +4: Superhuman
and they must sum to 2 unless the override flag is deliberately selected (which should only be done in special cases).
In addition to intrinsic characteristics, there exist **skills** which can be improved upon over time. These are as follows:
- **Dexterity**, or precision of a character's movements;
- and **elusion**, or ability to avoid certain obstacles.
For information on how these skills can be improved, see `/help character skillup`.
