# random clash
Used for determining the result of a melee clash
Based on a D20 roll with a vantage DC of 13

Vantage vaguely gives you the ability to follow through on your attack.
Unlike **/random shoot**, weapons directly influence the roll value rather than the DC (though flags still mainly affect the vantage DC).
+/- raise/lower your vantage DC respectively (so + is actually detrimental).
Advantage and disadvantage make you roll more times. Under advantage, you take the highest roll; under disadvantage, you take the lowest.

Flags:
- **A**dvantage
- **D**isadvantage
- **d**amaged weapon (+3 fumble chance, +1 base)
- **r**eplinished (-2)
- **t**ired (+3)
- d**i**soriented (+4)
